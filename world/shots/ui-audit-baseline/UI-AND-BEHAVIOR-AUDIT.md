## 2. What Works & Is Verified

1. **5-Scene Opening Flow (`app-flow.js`):**
   - Title, Loading, Create/Join, and Flower Field Lobby scenes mount cleanly inside `#haru-app-flow-root` (`z-index: 38`) and hide `#sculpted-hud-root` until `FLOW_STEPS.IN_GAME`.
   - Clicking `Enter` transitions `title -> loading` (`450ms`) `-> create_join`.
   - `#app-flow-phrase-input` uses `font-size: 1rem` (`16px`), preventing iOS/Android mobile auto-zoom on focus (`better-typography`).
   - Clicking `Start` in the Flower Field Lobby teleports both heroes to the Southern Flower Field trailhead `(0, 124)` facing north (`yaw = Math.PI`) and switches `camState.mode` to `'player'`.
2. **Progressive HUD & Interactive State Transitions (`sculpted-hud.js`):**
   - Bare start (`unlockedStage: 0, communeDiscovered: false`) hides `#sculpted-slot-e`, `#sculpted-slot-tab`, `#sculpted-slot-q`, and `#sculpted-commune-wrap`.
   - Clicking `#sh-emblem-primary` and `#sh-emblem-partner` opens `#sculpted-char-info-modal` for the respective hero (`Knight` `220/220` HP vs. `Mage` `90/90` HP).
   - All 3 modals (`#sculpted-char-info-modal`, `#sculpted-pause-modal`, `#sculpted-inscription-modal`) close via their primary Close/Resume button, backdrop click (`e.target === modal`), and `Escape` key.
   - Swapping active hero inside the Pause Modal (`#sculpted-pause-swap-btn`) or Character Info Modal (`#sculpted-char-swap-btn`) swaps the top/bottom life bar emblems and switches `#sculpted-attack-icon-slot` between `SVG_ATTACK_SWORD` (`Knight`) and `SVG_ATTACK_STAFF` (`Mage`).
   - Pressing `Tab` toggles `selectedElement` between `'ice'` and `'fire'`, switching `#sculpted-slot-e-icon` between `SVG_ICE_CRYSTAL_HEX` (`#1565c0`) and `SVG_FIRE_METEOR_HEX` (`#8f2412`) and `#sculpted-slot-tab-label` between `❄️ Ice` and `🔥 Fire`.
   - Switching to `tablet` profile adds `.profile-tablet` to `#sculpted-hud-root`, revealing `#sculpted-joystick-zone` (`x: 32, y: 638, w: 132, h: 132` at `1280×800`) without colliding with `#sculpted-powers-dock` (`x: 522, y: 708, w: 237, h: 72`) or `#sculpted-action-cluster` (`x: 1062, y: 604, w: 196, h: 176`).
