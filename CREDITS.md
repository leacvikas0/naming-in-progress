All models in these free downloads are present locally, including their textures
and buffers. Original ZIPs are retained in `library/_archives/`. Only selected
models should be loaded by the game. Paid editions and engine-specific shaders
are not included. See `game/handoff/ASSETS.md` for provenance and selection rules.

## Music

Two tracks — "Dungeon Adventure Title Theme" and "dungeon op 1" — are
AI-generated audio supplied by the project owner, and are integrated as the
title and combat themes. Loudness-normalized and loop-baked by
`game/audio/build-music.mjs`.
