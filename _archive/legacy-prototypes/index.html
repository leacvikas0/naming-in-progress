Staged Design Critique Loop

Checkpoint 1 · Trunk & 4-Order Skeleton

Initial Defect (v1): Trunk base extended to the bottom frame edge (y_img=1.0), primary crotch was too high, and tertiary twigs were straight segments.

Critique Fix (v2/v3): Grounded the 5-buttress root flare at Y=1.25 (y_img=0.84), matched the S-curve lean to the primary fork at Y=3.38, and added golden-angle (137.5°) gnarl and upward phototropism to all 146 branches.

Checkpoint 2 · Hero Leaf & 5-Petal Blossom

Initial Defect (v1): Adjacent flower petals overlapped into a fused morning-glory cone, and leaf rosettes drooped too steeply like umbrellas.

Critique Fix (v2): Sculpted 5 distinct elliptic-pointed star lobes separating cleanly at the centre with central longitudinal pleats, a flat petal plane (no raised trumpet cone), 5 golden stamen anthers in the violet eye, a 5-sepal green calyx cup tucked underneath, and 4-toothed serrated leaves with a 14° dihedral V-fold.

Checkpoint 3 · Canopy Phyllotaxis & Shaders

Initial Defect (v1): Uniform flower placement blanketed the entire canopy in pink dots and hid the interior woody forks; global sphere normals darkened the entire left wing.

Critique Fix (v2/v3): Grouped blossoms into 18 art-directed panicle bouquet zones resting on lush serrated leaf cushions, shifted petal hues to cool periwinkle-lavender (#b894dc → #e5ccf8), preserved interior branch sky-windows, and implemented sub-crown dual-normal Ghibli shading.
