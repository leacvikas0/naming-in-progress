Adapted source in this repository (Three.js game, not the reference project):

- game/src/ice-lance-vfx.js
  - `createCrystalGeometry` is adapted from
    `spellbook/src/assets/ProceduralGeometry.js` (`createCrystalGeometry`).
  - The injected ice shading in `createIceMaterial` is a reduced adaptation of
    `spellbook/src/materials/IceMaterial.js` (thickness tint, fresnel rim, rime,
    birth flash, glints). The reference's noise library, environment probe,
    disruption hooks and editor plumbing were not ported.
  - Cast orchestration/timing (front travel, per-record eruption, lean, crown,
    sink) follows the behaviour of `spellbook/src/abilities/IceAbility.js`.
- game/src/ice-lance-config.js
  - Tuning values are adapted from `spellbook/src/config/abilities/ice.js`.
