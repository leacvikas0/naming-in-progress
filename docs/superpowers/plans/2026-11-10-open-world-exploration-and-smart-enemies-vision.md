## 1. Core World & Exploration Pillars

1. **A Much Larger, Genuinely Explorative World:**
   - The true vision for *Amarante* is a **much larger, expansive world** where the 3+ Sacred Stones are **genuinely hard to find**.
   - **Finding the Stones IS the Game:** Players should have to explore the world, read environmental clues, investigate distant landmarks, hidden groves, ruins, and secluded corners to discover the stones themselves—never following hand-holding GPS arrows or overly obvious UI markers.

2. **Powerful Novel-Like Pacing (Breathing Room Without Going Soft):**
   - The game should read like a great novel with distinct beats: quiet moments when you actually discover a hidden stone and read its carved poetry or speak to the God of Love, contrasted with **intense, high-stakes combat**.
   - Do **not** water the game down with overly long 60-second dead breaks—keep the tension, danger, and momentum powerful while ensuring enemies don't cheaply glitch-interrupt you mid-poem once you've earned your moment at a stone.

3. **Wider Enemy Diversity & Smarter Tactical AI (Full KayKit + Native Roster):**
   - Leverage the full breadth of our KayKit and rigged character library (`library/kaykit-skeletons/`, `library/enemy-candidates/`, and future expansions) so different regions and hunting packs feature distinct visual silhouettes, weapon kits, and coordinated tactics:
     - Frontline shield phalanxes (`Skeleton_Warrior`, `BlackKnight`) that force flanking, parrying, or elemental guard-breaks.
     - Fast flankers and ambushers (`Skeleton_Rogue`, `Werewolf_Wolf`, `OrcRaider`) that punish exposed casters and react to threat/kill streaks.
     - Ranged artillery and hybrid switchers (`Skeleton_Mage`, `PlantWarrior`, `Necromancer`) that control space, summon reinforcements on fair timers, and force active dodging and shield interception.
     - Towering regional guardians and multi-phase golems (`OrcBrute`, `FrostGolem`, `Skeleton_Golem`).
