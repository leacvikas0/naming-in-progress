# Sacred Verse & Lapidary Inscription Craft (`Amarante`)

## 1. Purpose & Aesthetic Standard

Sacred inscriptions in **Amarante** (*The Unfading Bloom*) are diegetic stone carvings weathered into limestone well rims, sunken golden grove medallions, and hidden cliff-wall sanctuaries. When two players discover a stone and read its verse together, they must feel as if they are deciphering **ancient, aching romantic mythos**—never a tutorial popup, never a sing-song nursery riddle (`"My first is X, my second is Y"`), and never synthetic LLM poetry.

This skill synthesizes established poetic craft from:
- **`verse-craft` (`danjdewhurst/story-skills`)**: Explicit stress scansion, natural syntax preservation, zero filler padding, and ban on overused rhyme pairs.
- **`avoid-ai-writing`**: Elimination of synthetic abstractions, theatrical punctuation habits, and stock LLM phrasing.
- **Classical & Modernist Lapidary Traditions**:
  - **Sappho & Greek Epigraphs (`Poets.org` Sapphic / Epigraph canon)**: Forceful trochaic/dactylic openings, fragments that carry physical ache in spare syllables, and direct address to the passerby or god.
  - **Gerard Manley Hopkins (*Sprung Rhythm*, *Inscape*, & *Saxon Diction*)**: Stress-timed propulsion (`3–4` heavy accents per line), compound tactile nouns, Anglo-Saxon monosyllables (`>75%` concrete Germanic root vocabulary), and audible consonant chime.
  - **Rainer Maria Rilke (*Dinggedichte* / Thing-Poems) & Natalie Ann Holborow**: Rooting emotion inside physical matter—iron, cold stone, briar sap, frost-splinter, bruised knee, and breath—rather than naming abstract feelings.
  - **Old English Hemistich & Medial Caesura (`·`)**: Dividing key lines into two balanced rhythmic half-lines separated by a carved mid-dot (`·`) or natural breath pause so the stone reads like an ancient vow.
  - ***Elden Ring*, *Shadow of the Colossus*, & *Hollow Knight* Diegetic Lore**: Encoding mechanical truth inside mythic law and tragedy rather than instructional imperatives.

## 2. Non-Negotiable Stone-Carving & Layout Constraints

Every sacred inscription in the world is rendered into 3D carved stone (`EB Garamond` vector typography / normal-mapped stone geometry) and HUD inspection tablets. Every poem MUST obey these exact physical dimensions:

1. **Line Count**: Strictly **8 lines** per inscription (`2` four-line stanzas / quatrains, or `4` carved distichs).
2. **Character Width**:
   - **Target range**: **`24–34` characters per line** (including spaces and punctuation).
   - **Hard ceiling**: **`<= 36` characters per line** (never exceed 36 characters on any line, or the 3D carved glyphs will crowd the stone bezel).
   - **Minimum floor**: **`>= 20` characters per line** so the tablet block looks balanced and monumental.
3. **Carved Caesura & Punctuation**:
   - Use restrained, classical stone punctuation: commas, semicolons, em-dashes sparingly (`<= 1` per poem), or the lapidary mid-dot caesura (`·`) to mark a sacred turn or breath between half-lines.
   - Never use exclamation marks (`!`) or modern chat punctuation (`...`) on ancient stone.

## 3. Hard Bans: AI Clichés, Nursery Riddles & Forced Rhymes

### 3A. Banned AI Vocabulary (Immediate Rejection)
Never use any of the following synthetic LLM poetic defaults:
- **Banned Nouns**: `tapestry`, `symphony`, `beacon`, `testament`, `mosaic`, `crucible`, `dance` (as metaphor, e.g., *"dance of shadows"*), `echo` / `echoes`, `whisper` / `whispers`, `realm`, `journey`, `embrace`, `destiny`, `harmony`, `void`, `abyss`.
- **Banned Adjectives/Adverbs**: `ethereal`, `celestial`, `unwavering`, `boundless`, `transcendent`, `luminous`, `resplendent`, `forevermore`, `everlasting`, `intertwined`.

### 3B. Banned Riddle Templates
- **No Syllable-Charade Templates**: Never write `"My first is..."`, `"My second is..."`, `"Bind the first unto the second"`, or `"What am I?"`.
- **No Instructional UI Verbs**: Never write `"Press"`, `"Hold thy shield button"`, `"Speak into the mic"`, `"Stand within 4 meters"`, or `"Cast thy spell"`.
- **No Sing-Song Nursery Couplets**: Avoid bouncing `da-DUM da-DUM da-DUM da-DUM` iambic tetrameter in `AABB` with predictable end-stopped clauses on every single line.

### 3C. Banned Overused Rhyme Pairs (`verse-craft` Standard)
Per `verse-craft/references/rhyme.md`, the following exhausted rhyme pairs read as automatic defaults and are **banned**:
- `heart / apart` (or `heart / start / part`)
- `love / above` (or `love / dove`)
- `fire / desire`
- `night / light / bright / sight`
- `day / way / away`
- `pain / again / rain`
- `soul / whole`
- `tears / years / fears`
- `sky / high / fly / die`
- `free / sea / me / thee`

**What to use instead**:
- **Concrete noun/verb rhymes** (`rim / dim`, `shaft / craft`, `thorn / sworn`, `husk / dusk`, `knees / freeze`, `ward / cord`, `stone / bone`, `bloom / tomb`, `lip / grip`, `frost / lost`, `name / claim`, `skin / kin`).
- **Rich slant / consonance rhymes (`B'`)**: Matching terminal consonants with shifted vowels (`steel / fall`, `wound / wind`, `stone / crown`, `breath / earth`, `blood / blade`, `grief / leaf`) to give the verse an older, harder, pre-modern ring.
- **Envelope or Alternating Schemes (`ABAB CDCD`, `ABBA CDDC`, or `ABCB DEFE`)**: Prefer interlaced rhyme (`ABAB` or `ABCB`) or slant-linked couplets with enjambment over mechanical `AABB` nursery couplets.

## 4. Prosody, Meter & Scansion Rules

1. **Accentual-Trochaic or Sprung 3-Beat / 4-Beat Line**:
   - Because each line is compact (`24–34` characters, `5–8` words), a **3-beat or tight 4-beat accentual/sprung line** fits the stone width naturally without crowding.
   - Starting lines on a stressed syllable (trochaic/dactylic opening or headless iamb, as in Sappho and Hopkins) creates immediate lapidary gravity.
2. **Zero Padding (`verse-craft` Hard Rule)**:
   - Never insert `did + verb` (*"did fall"*, *"did speak"*), `so`, `very`, `oh`, `unto`, or doubled filler adjectives just to fill a beat.
3. **Zero Wrenched Syntax**:
   - Never invert natural English clause order solely to force a rhyme word to the end of a line. Every sentence must read with clean, dignified syntax when spoken aloud.
4. **Enjambment & Volta**:
   - Across the 8 lines, at least 2–3 lines should enjamb across the line break so the thought pours from stone course to stone course rather than stopping dead every 30 characters.
   - Place a clear **volta (turn)** at Line 5 (the opening of the second quatrain), shifting from the mythic hazard/curse (Lines 1–4) to the secret covenant/act that transforms it (Lines 5–8).

## 5. The Principle of Oblique Mechanical Truth

A great sacred poem in *Amarante* never hands the player an instruction manual; it states a **mythic law of the valley** that players must interpret and test together:

| Mechanical Truth | Amateur Tutorial / Cheap Riddle (BANNED) | Sacred Mythic Obliqueness (REQUIRED) |
|---|---|---|
| **Shared Shield & Close Tether against the Freezing Bolt** | *"Stand close together when the ice arrow falls and hold block with your shield to protect your partner."* | Speak of the well's iron lip, the white star-javelin / winter's shaft that hunts divided shadows, and how one raised iron ward must roof two overlapping breaths. |
| **Calling `Artorious` for the Beloved, Never Selfishly (`heal me`)** | *"Say Artorious and ask to heal your partner, because if you ask for yourself he will punish you."* | Speak of the gold-veined briar stone where `Artorious` keeps his blind vigil—deaf to the beggar pleading for his own skin, waking only when a mouth names him to ransom the other soul. |
| **Calling `Artorious` in the Final Hush When Both Fall at the Briar** | *"When the tree boss knocks you both down in the storm, pray to Artorious for your partner to revive."* | Speak of the thorn-king's gale bringing both lovers to their knees in the ash—and how, before the dark seals their mouths, naming `Artorious` in a final vow for one's beloved turns the briar-grave into the unfading bloom. |

## 6. Pre-Commit Verification Checklist for Every Sacred Poem

Before saving any poem to `world/src/ui/sacred-poems-unspoiled.js` or `worker/scripture-catalog.js`, run a verification script to confirm:
- [ ] **Exact Line Count**: `lines.length === 8` for every site (`site1`, `site2`, `site3`).
- [ ] **Character Width**: Every single line satisfies `20 <= line.length <= 36` (with majority in the `24–34` sweet spot).
- [ ] **Zero Banned Words**: None of the banned AI clichés (`tapestry`, `symphony`, `whisper`, `echo`, `beacon`, `testament`, etc.) appear in any line.
- [ ] **Zero Banned Rhyme Pairs**: None of `heart/apart`, `love/above`, `fire/desire`, `night/light`, `day/way`, `pain/again`, `soul/whole`, `tears/years` are used as end-rhymes.
- [ ] **Scansion & Aloud Test**: Every line has `3–4` natural spoken stresses, zero `did + verb` padding, and zero inverted rhyme syntax.
- [ ] **Spoiler Hygiene**: Never print, quote, or log the poem lines in terminal output, test logs, or agent messages.
