Haru-machi | Divine Prayer & Inscription Design
Conversation handoff  ·  6 November 2026  ·  one document + one generated concept image
Current working plan
The purpose is affectionate co-op play: the two players say cute, silly or desperate prayers aloud; an audio-capable LLM judges expression and context; the God grants proportional power or punishes deliberate disrespect.
No real-time divine voice synthesis in gameplay. Responses are prerecorded with an offline TTS model, then selected by clip ID. Players are already on a call together; no in-game prayer-audio relay is required.
Three discovery spots: (1) small accessible clue at the existing village well, (2) long secluded inscribed stone wall behind an existing house near the mill/lake, (3) large circular ground inscription uncovered when the Demon Tree dies and its roots retract.
Only the name discovery is a small required mystery. Keep lettering short and readable by walking around, without floating text panels or dense quest chains. Use image-generated inscriptions integrated via stone materials; optional 3D geometry for the heart motif.
The God responds to near-correct, harmlessly wrong, or knowingly insulting invocations differently. The memorable deliberate-mockery penalty targets the OTHER player (the beloved).
Never expose the actual secret name or puzzle answers. Any names/quotes visible in concepts are fictional placeholders, not canon.
